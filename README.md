# MaizeLink GPAA
### Connecting GPAA Farmers to Market, One Phone at a Time
By: JAMES NYADUNG THANKO| Power Learn Project - Pitch Deck
Tagline: No more wasted maize. No more hungry buyers.

## Slide 2: The Problem
- Farmers in GPAA grow a lot of maize, but cannot find buyers
- Maize rots in stores because there is no market link
- Middlemen buy very cheap and sell very expensive
- Mothers and schools cannot find affordable maize flour

## Slide 3: The Solution
MaizeLink - A simple mobile app for farmers
- Farmer posts: "I have 10 bags, 500 SSP per bag, in Pibor"
- Buyer sees price, location, and orders in the app
- Works on low phones, low internet, English + local language

## Slide 4: Target Market
- Small Farmers: 5,000+ in Pibor, Akobo, etc.
- Mothers, Schools, Small Shops / Traders
- Market Size: 1,000 farmers x 10 bags = 10,000 bags per season

## Slide 5: Product / How It Works
1. Farmer Registers - Name, village, amount
2. Farmer Posts Maize - Photo, price, location
3. Buyer Orders - Click Buy, connect by call / SMS
Features: Price list, SMS alerts, offline mode, Cash / Mobile Money

## Slide 6: Business Model
- Commission: 5% on each bag sold
- Delivery Fee, Premium Listing, Ads
- Example: 1,000 bags x 1,000 SSP = 50,000 SSP commission

## Slide 7: Marketing Strategy
- Demo in markets and churches, women groups, schools
- Radio in Pibor, 20 youth ambassadors, WhatsApp groups

## Slide 8: Competition / Why We Win
- Middlemen cheat farmers. We give fair price.
- Facebook / WhatsApp: No price, no trust. We have real price + location.
- Other Apps: For big cities. Ours is made for GPAA.
- Our Edge: We are from GPAA, we speak the language.
